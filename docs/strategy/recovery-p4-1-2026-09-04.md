## Why P4.1 exists

The P4 source architecture was internally consistent, but three older release audits still encoded pre-recovery assumptions:

1. `internal-link-audit.mjs` rejected every link to a `HOLD_NOINDEX` page, including the intentional legal/utility pages `/terms/` and `/privacy-policy/`. These pages are deliberately `200 + noindex,follow` and may be linked from the footer; they are not topical authority targets.
2. `content-copy-audit.mjs` compared later P1/P3 sanitized rewrites against legacy approved-copy documents that still contain superseded SEO/editorial text. P4.1 does not restore that text. Instead it pins the 17 intentionally rewritten source files by exact SHA-256 in `reports/content/recovery-approved-copy-baseline.json`; any later source mutation invalidates the approval.
3. `cutover-audit.mjs` still expected the pre-P2 counts `46 redirects` and `57 INDEX pages`, and expected seven originally-held historical survivors to remain HOLD forever. P4.1 records those seven approved P2 dispositions in `migration/approved/survivor-reconciliations.json` and validates their exact INDEX/REDIRECT outcome and redirect targets.

## Safety invariants retained

- Terms and Privacy are the only explicit noindex utility-link exceptions.
- Generated topical/cluster links must still resolve to `INDEX + 200` pages.
- Recovery copy approval is an exact source-file SHA pin, not a dynamic self-approval.
- Original 63 survivor decisions remain preserved; seven later recovery decisions are tracked as explicit reconciliations rather than rewriting history.
- Redirect/GONE/canonical/content-status checks remain active.
- No production-visible SEO/migration text was reintroduced.
